# Taj Tyre Works - Customer Portal User Manual

Welcome to the **Taj Tyre Works Customer Portal**. This guide provides end-to-end instructions for accessing services, tracking active orders, and managing your loyalty profile.

---

## 1. Landing Page & Interactive Widgets

When visiting the application, you can access several helpful widgets on the homepage:
- **3D Tyre Interactive Canvas**: Drag, click, and inspect tyre models in high fidelity. Click to explode the 3D components and click again to assemble them.
- **Tyre Size Finder**: Input your vehicle brand or click tyre dimensions to get instant size and fitment recommendations.
- **Shelf Showroom & Comparison Tool**: Superimpose and inspect tyre brands side-by-side to compare warranties, specifications, and prices.

---

## 2. Customer Authentication (OTP-backed)

To enter your secure dashboard:
1. Navigate to the **Login Page** and select the **Customer** tab.
2. Enter your registered **10-digit mobile number** (e.g., `9199936181`) and click **Request Verification Code**.
3. A mock OTP is automatically generated and displayed on-screen for local testing (in production, this links to an SMS gateway).
4. Enter the **6-digit verification code** to sign in. The session remains active for 30 minutes of inactivity.

---

## 3. Customer Dashboard Features

### 3.1. Overview & Service Status
- The homepage shows your active queue appointments, primary vehicle parameters, and low-stock alerts.
- Use the quick support link to message our support desk directly via WhatsApp.

### 3.2. Warehouse Inventory Lookup
- Navigate to the **Stock Catalog** in the sidebar.
- Search and filter tyres by categories (e.g., Car Tyres, Wheels, Tubes) or manufacturers.
- Click **Inquire Price** to launch a pre-populated inquiry on WhatsApp.

### 3.3. Loyalty Cards & Rewards
- Navigate to **Loyalty Card**.
- View your interactive 20-box visit grid showing check-ins stamped by the garage receptionist.
- Scroll down to view unlocked discount coupons (e.g., "10% Off Balancing", "Free Nitrogen Top-up") with copyable promo codes.

### 3.4. Appointment Scheduling
- Navigate to **Book Appointment**.
- Enter your vehicle number, select service type (Fitting, Alignment, Balancing, Nitrogen), select date, and pick a time slot.
- The screen dynamically displays queue workload warning tags based on active garage bookings.

### 3.5. Warranty & Vehicle History
- **Warranty Center**: Tracks serial numbers, invoice reference IDs, and days-remaining countdown gauges.
- **Service History**: Interactive vertical timeline showing every check-in date, plate number, mileage counter, and receptionist comments.

### 3.6. Invoices & Digital Receipts
- Navigate to **Invoices** to view receipts.
- Click the view icon to load a complete GST receipt detailing CGST (14% tyres, 9% services), SGST (14% tyres, 9% services), and final totals.
- Digital signatures from the workshop manager are rendered directly onto the print-ready invoice.
